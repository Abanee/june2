Methodology Pillars

The Science of Speed & Flow

Unlike generic coaching, our curriculum is engineered around six technical pillars designed to transform your efficiency and speed in the water.

Pillar 01

Body Hydrodynamics

The foundation of all speed is minimizing drag. We focus on neutral head position, core engagement, and horizontal body alignment to help you slide through the water effortlessly.

Streamlining Core Control Drag Reduction
See Levels →
Pillar 02

Propulsive Leverage

Convert raw muscle power into forward velocity. We isolate the early vertical forearm (EVF), catch positioning, and high-elbow pull-throughs to maximize your grip on the water.

Catch & Pull EVF Technique Power Output
See Levels →
Pillar 03

Rhythmic Breathing

Breathe without disrupting your balance. Master bilateral breathing to distribute muscular work evenly, maintain a stable swim line, and build superior aerobic capacity.

Bilateral Airway Spinal Rotation Aerobic Capacity
See Levels →
Pillar 04

Explosive Transitions

The race is won off the walls. Learn the physics of high-velocity start blocks, tight flip-turn compression, and streamlined underwater dolphin kicks to carry maximum speed.

Dive Starts Tumble Turns Streamlines
See Levels →
Pillar 05

Kick Synchronization

Stabilize and propel. We dial in your kick tempo—from the energy-saving 2-beat kick for distance to the high-cadence 6-beat sprint kick—to elevate your hips and drive rotation.

Kick Tempo Hip Elevation Sprint Kick
See Levels →
Pillar 06

Pacing & Conditioning

Develop critical endurance. Train your nervous system to sustain active speeds, regulate lactic acid build-up, and utilize visualization techniques to optimize race efficiency.

Endurance Lactic Threshold Mental Focus
See Levels →
