Our Story

A pool, a coach, and a promise to every parent

Current Aquatics Academy was founded in 2010 by a national-level swimmer who watched too many adults avoid the water out of fear learned as children. The goal was simple: build a place where water confidence came before technique, and where safety was never negotiable.

What began as after-school lessons in a single community pool grew, batch by batch, into a full Olympic-standard facility. Along the way, our coaches stayed close to one idea — every swimmer, regardless of age or ability, deserves patient, professional instruction in a genuinely safe environment.

Today, that same philosophy shapes every class we run, from a four-year-old's first splash to a competitive athlete's final lap before a state final. We measure our growth not just in square footage, but in the thousands of confident swimmers we've sent back into the world.

Trust & Safety

Safety & training standards

This is the section every parent reads first — and the one we take the most seriously. Here's exactly how we keep every swimmer safe, every session.

Certified Lifeguards

On every deck, every session, no exceptions.

Water Quality Monitoring

Chemical and clarity checks every two hours.

First Aid Station

Fully stocked station staffed during all hours.

Emergency Response

Drilled protocols and direct lines to local EMS.

CCTV Monitoring

Full-campus coverage reviewed by our safety team.

Regular Pool Cleaning

Daily deep cleaning outside of operating hours.

Sign-in/sign-out and verified guardian pickup only.
