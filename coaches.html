Head Coach

Leading from the deck

12+ Years experience

Arjun Mehta

Head Coach & Academy Director

A former national-level medalist, Arjun has spent over a decade turning nervous first-timers into confident swimmers and promising juniors into state and national qualifiers. He leads our competitive program and oversees coaching standards across every batch at the academy.

His teaching philosophy is simple: technique before speed, and confidence before competition. Arjun believes every swimmer — regardless of age or ambition — deserves the same attention to detail he gives his top competitive athletes.

Fluent in English, Hindi, and Tamil, Arjun works closely with families to build training plans that respect each swimmer's pace while keeping an eye on long-term progress.

Teaching Methodology

How our coaches build every swimmer

A consistent, six-step process every coach follows — whether the swimmer is four years old or training for a national final.

Skill Assessment Training

Every swimmer is evaluated in the water to establish a true starting point.

Personalized Training Plan

Coaches design a plan around the swimmer's pace, goals, and comfort level.

Technique Development

Stroke mechanics are broken down and rebuilt drill by drill.

Endurance & Confidence

Structured sets grow stamina while keeping the water a safe, positive place.
