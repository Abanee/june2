Why Current

Built for swimmers who take it seriously

Every detail of our program — the pool, the coaching, the tracking — is designed around one outcome: swimmers who improve, safely and fast.

Certified Coaches

Every coach is ASCA / Swim England certified, with a minimum of 5 years on-deck experience.

Olympic Pool

Train in our heated, 10-lane, 50m Olympic-standard pool with real-time lap timing systems.

Safety First

Certified lifeguards on every deck, 1:6 max coach-to-student ratio, and CPR-trained staff.

Flexible Batches

Early morning, evening, and weekend batches — built to work around school and office hours.

Progress Tracking

Every swimmer gets a digital progress card — stroke, endurance, and timing tracked every term.

Modern Equipment

Underwater cameras, resistance bands, and pace clocks used in every session, every level.
