Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Most common questions before enrolling — answered. Still have one? Use the form above.

What age groups do you accept?

We welcome swimmers from age 4 upward. Our Kids program covers ages 4–12, and adult batches are open to all ages with no upper limit.

How do I enroll in a batch?

Simply fill in the form above or call us directly. We'll confirm availability, share batch details, and guide you through the registration steps.

Can I try a free trial class first?

Yes! Every new student gets one free trial session. No equipment needed — just arrive in comfortable clothing and we'll take care of the rest.

What are the batch timing options?

We run early morning (5:30 AM), morning, afternoon, and evening batches on weekdays, plus dedicated weekend sessions for families and professionals.

Women's taught by women coaches?

Absolutely. All Women's-only batches are conducted exclusively by our certified women coaches in a private, comfortable pool environment.

Do you track swimmer progress?

Yes — every swimmer gets a digital progress card updated each term, tracking stroke technique, endurance, timing, and overall confidence milestones.
